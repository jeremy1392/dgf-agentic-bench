\begin{abstract}
\noindent Can an AI review an enterprise project, identify the problems, and make a justified
decision at every governance checkpoint? We introduce DGF-Bench and connect this bounded experiment
to a broader thesis about the automation of Digital Governance Frameworks (DGF). The generator
constructs 300 fictional projects, their architectures, documents, and simulated records: 100 Buy,
100 Integrate, and 100 Build cases. Agents inspect allowed evidence, apply explicit policies,
check mandates, submit structured decisions, and pass their reviews downstream. A separate
evaluator scores decisions, findings, proposed actions, evidence, and authorization against the
case reference. The tested models are Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-5.6 Luna, and DeepSeek v4.1 Flash,
accessed through OpenRouter under common task and output limits.

There are 899 evaluable model--case runs out of 900; one provider failure is excluded. Strict
gate success is 94.98\% for Gemini, 83.29\% for Luna, and 74.18\% for DeepSeek. Requiring every
gate of a project to succeed reduces those rates to 76.92\%, 42.33\%, and 24.67\%. All 85
Gemini strict failures concern evidence conformity, despite correct dispositions throughout its
evaluable runs. Total recorded API cost is USD~87.02. These observations distinguish choosing
the correct decision from completing an auditable review under explicit rules and accessible
structured facts. They support the prospect of substantial automation of the represented review
tasks and, if performance transfers to operational settings, reduced human effort and staffing.
Autonomous remediation and human equivalence are not measured.

The theoretical contribution treats gates as information-transforming contracts and identifies
the implementation role of Forward Deployed Engineers. A complete labor account explains why,
in a separate synthetic 140-FTE example, identical automation coverage can leave 44 or 169 FTE.
The hypothesis of 80\% fewer required DGF FTE by 2033, including support at comparable output
and quality, remains untested by the benchmark. Complete substitution is a stronger, undated
conjecture. Model measurements, workforce scenarios, and prospective claims are kept separate.
\end{abstract}
