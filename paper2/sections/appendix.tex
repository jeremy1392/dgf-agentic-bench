The dimensional identity is valid at $p=0$ or $p=1$ using only the group that exists.
Group-relative ratios require positive baseline group means. The constraint $0\leq w\leq1$
does not constrain $a$ or $u$ to be at most one: review or exception work can increase after
deployment. Fixed support is not eliminated by multiplying every term by a volume adjustment.
If volumes change, separate fixed support from variable review effort before standardization.

An activity should be counted exactly once regardless of departmental ownership or supplier
boundary. For example, a specialist's correction after an agent approval is either exception
handling or additional rework under the declared coding rule, not both. A shared evaluation
team's hours are counted once before allocation across workflows. A repeat gate visit may be
included in review volume or in per-project effort; the study must choose a consistent basis.
Elapsed waiting time and active human time are distinct, although exclusive monitoring may
consume human capacity. Record the rule used for that case.

\section{Residual selection: the short result}
\label{app:selection}

\begin{proposition}
Let $T\geq0$ be integrable baseline handling time with mean $h_0>0$. Let $J$ indicate
assignment to human exception handling, $p=\Pr(J=1)>0$, and $e(T)=\Pr(J=1\mid T)$.
The exception group's baseline labor share satisfies
\[
w=\frac{\mathbb{E}[TJ]}{h_0}
 =p+\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(T,e(T))}{h_0}.
\]
Consequently it exceeds the case share exactly when routing is positively associated with
baseline effort. If $e$ is nondecreasing, this covariance is nonnegative.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Conditional expectation gives $\mathbb{E}[TJ]=\mathbb{E}[Te(T)]$ and
$\mathbb{E}[e(T)]=p$, giving the identity. For independent copies $T,T'$,
$2\operatorname{Cov}(T,e(T))=\mathbb{E}[(T-T')(e(T)-e(T'))]$.
The integrand is nonnegative when $e$ is nondecreasing.
\end{proof}

No claim about the conditional variance of exception times follows from the proposition.
Estimating $w$ requires baseline hours under a frozen routing partition, not just a count of
cases later failed by a model. Changes in the routing rule require a new partition or a
documented reweighting. The identity supports measurement of concentration of remaining work;
it does not predict its size without those measurements.

\section{Verification and follow-up record}

The original strict outcomes remain the primary results of the existing experiment. A future
semantic audit should retain item identifiers, observed evidence paths, cited claims, independent
labels, disagreement reasons, adjudicated labels, and sampling probabilities. Release the
rubric and analysis version with the results. Keep the lexical and semantic scores side by
side so that differences can be traced to particular findings rather than only to a revised
headline rate.

The field protocol must record registration identifier, enrollment date, eligible population,
baseline provenance, operating boundary, quality thresholds, primary labor outcome, uncertainty
procedure, and missing-data rules before outcome analysis. None of those identifiers is invented
for this manuscript. If historical records cannot support the 2026 baseline, a new prospective
study must state its own dates and hypothesis explicitly.
