# Proposed human, agent, and rules baseline

**Status: design only. No participants, collected outcomes, or preregistration identifier.**

## Question and conditions

Compare unaided qualified humans, agents, and humans reviewing agent drafts on the same information contract. The deterministic control has now been executed on the original 300 dossiers (100% strict gate/route success). Include it again on independently adjudicated held-out cases; no comparator may read hidden reference answers. Separately evaluate document-only inputs to measure reliance on structured facts. Do not present an answer-key oracle as a substantive baseline.

## Before collection

1. Obtain the applicable ethics review, consent, and data-handling arrangements. Record participant expertise and compensation.
2. Create independently adjudicated held-out cases and a separate training set. Document ambiguous policies and acceptable escalation outcomes.
3. Freeze the output contract, budgets, adjudication rubric, noninferiority margins, primary outcomes, exclusion rules, analysis code, and model identities.
4. Choose case and reviewer counts with a power/precision analysis accounting for case pairing, reviewer clustering, and the frequency of critical events. This document does not declare an adequate sample size without those inputs.
5. Register the design and disclose prior inspection of the September 2026 benchmark.

## Assignment and measurement

Counterbalance condition and order; never assign the same case to the same human twice across unaided and assisted arms. Preserve full route context for route-level outcomes. Use structured forms for human submissions and separate form-entry time from investigation. Mask condition and model identity from independent adjudicators where practical.

Record active investigation, draft verification, correction, escalation, outside-specialist assistance, and finalization time. Record elapsed cycle time separately. Retain unsuccessful and incomplete attempts and report their handling before analysis.

Primary outcomes: independently adjudicated decisions, false approvals, critical misses, semantic evidence support, and total active human minutes. Secondary outcomes: exact format conformity, cycle time, and cost. Publish quality and time jointly; faster incorrect reviews do not establish an efficiency gain.

## Analysis and reporting

Use a model accommodating paired cases and reviewer effects or an appropriate multi-level resampling design. Estimate differences with uncertainty and apply the registered quality margins before interpreting time savings. Publish all attempts, exclusions, inter-rater agreement, disagreement resolutions, timing definitions, and the code used. Do not generalize synthetic human comparisons to enterprise FTE without deployment measurements.
