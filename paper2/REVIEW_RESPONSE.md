## Latest review: additional work completed

The reviewer correctly identifies the missing causal comparison of policy/evidence conditions. Offline analyses cannot substitute for that experiment. The original task is near saturation for the strongest tested endpoint and its rule-engine control; that observation is now explicit.

- Exact conditional-approval uses are 398 for Gemini, 353 for Luna, 202 for DeepSeek. The previous 139 was only Gemini label-changing approvals. Matched non-General eligibility gives 391 common opportunities; rejected requests are separately reported.
- All 205 support entries in evidence-failed Procurement findings cite observed sources. DeepSeek supplies 25 exact CSV rows across 23 occurrences; those sources are excluded by the original instructed contract. This diagnoses contract conformity, not proof of false factual assertions or automatic semantic success.
- The same post-hoc structural method is applied to all 135 repetition runs.
- The 300-case source inventory distinguishes physical presence, matching values, source availability and uncertain decoding. Two generator omissions are demonstrated; a new extension adds targeted CSVs in three development cases without changing the original corpus.
- A source-location citation prototype and tests are provided. The frozen scorer is unchanged. A future source-only adapter must also block the indirect snapshot return from request_evidence.
- DGF is expanded, the obsolete workforce-figure caption and revision wording are removed, the abstract states the full normalization behavior, and overlapping audit subsections are merged.

Extra restrictive decisions can cause delay or rework, but the claim that they are only a cost and never a danger is not measured here. No numerical score or grade guarantees acceptance. Controlled model comparisons remain necessary to test decisions from less prepared evidence. The author has excluded independent human evaluation from the current programme; no measured human-equivalence claim is made. Preparation is not described as completed evidence.

## Current scope decision

The study targets the capability of LLM agents to execute decision tasks normally assigned to people at governance gates. It evaluates dossier review, policy application, evidence, authority, and handoffs. Independent human evaluation and recruitment are outside the current programme. Earlier human/semantic protocols are retained as archived proposals, not active next steps. This scope does not supply an empirical comparison with human accuracy.
